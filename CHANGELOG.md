# Changelog

User-visible changes, newest first. One line per change, in plain words.

## Unreleased

Everything below is built and tested but **not yet walked on a phone** (`docs/tasks/RELEASE-CANDIDATE.md`, the definition of pass); nothing here is released.

* Importing a YouTube Music playlist no longer searches YouTube for every song: the songs play, and are saved on your server, from the video the playlist points at. Before, a 288-song import made about 50 searches in its first five minutes, a song you tapped before its turn was searched at the tap, and saving a 200-song playlist took about an hour and a half instead of about forty minutes.
* If the app is closed or killed while a song is playing (the system ending it, a swipe from the recent apps), it now comes back within 2 seconds of where you were. Before, it could be up to 15 seconds off, and a jump you had just made inside the song was forgotten: seeking to 2:28 and being killed 2 seconds later brought the song back at 4:01.
* Updating the server (the command encore update) after a small change now downloads a few megabytes instead of about 240: the server's libraries are kept in a layer of their own that only changes when the libraries do. On a slow line the last update took over 7 minutes.
* The installer and the install guide no longer say your music folder is "only read". What is true: Encore never changes your own files; songs you keep from the web are saved in a folder called Encore inside it, and the installer now says so when it asks where your music is.
* Behind the scenes: when many people use one server at the same moment, looking at your library or your playlists no longer waits for other people's changes to finish. With twenty simulated people the slowest wait fell from about 6 seconds to under a second; two changes still take turns, but each is short and nothing slow happens inside one.
* Behind the scenes: the server writes about 18 times faster (each change no longer pays for closing and re-opening its database), so many people changing things at once wait less.
* After a list has played to its end, pressing Play (on the album page, the mini player, the lock screen or a headset) now starts it over from the first song, and when it ends again the player stops and shows Play. Before, Play replayed the last song once and then showed Pause with no sound.
* On the server, pressing Download on a playlist or on your liked songs can now ask the server for the songs it does not have yet, ahead of other people's imports; a single tap on a song still comes first, and a long download can no longer push another person's tap behind it. Saving a release and Retry follow the same rule.
* On your phone, pressing Download on a playlist or on your liked songs now asks your server to get the songs it does not have yet, ahead of other people's imports, and the phone keeps checking while the app is open.
* Under a download (in Library, on the playlist or album page, and in Settings > Downloads & storage) you now see how far BOTH stages have got: "12 of 217 on this phone · 40 ready on your server", "Waiting its turn on your server", "your server is resting until 21:40", or why your phone waits ("waiting for Wi-Fi", "waiting for a connection", "waiting for your server", or that downloads are paused because your storage limit is reached or your phone is almost full), with a bar of two shades. Songs that did not come are named with a reason and a Retry button. The notification no longer says "Downloads finished" when your server still has songs to hand over.
* When a song cannot be saved to the server, a friend now sees a short plain reason ("Not found.", "Needs an age check.") and never the server's internal error text or folder names; the person who runs the server still sees the cause.
* The server can now say how far it is with keeping every playlist, album and your liked songs for download, in one answer: how many songs it has ready, how many it is fetching or has queued, and which ones it could not get and why (to you as the person who runs the server, with the cause; to a friend, in plain words). The app does not show it yet (a phone can also ask to be told as it changes, so the numbers move by themselves). A song that failed to save is no longer forgotten after a week while someone still has it in a playlist or liked.
* Offline, the app now keeps showing what you cannot play after the "You're offline" line has gone: Home, the Library and the song lists show it grey with "Not downloaded", Home lists what is on your phone under "Downloaded", and it all goes away by itself when the connection is back. When Android finds internet but your server does not answer, the line says "Can't reach your server."; with no internet (no data, a sign-in page) it still says "You're offline". A tap on a song that is not downloaded says which of the two it is.
* The first time you press Download, Encore asks once, in plain words, whether it may show how far your downloads have got (Android 13 and newer); say "Not now" and it never asks again, and the download goes ahead either way. No news or alerts are sent unless you switch them on in Settings.
* Songs the music server could not file under an album no longer show up as an album called "[Unknown Album]" with a grey cover: Home leaves that out, and the lists, the artist page, the player and the lock screen show them as songs, with the one name "Songs without an album" where a name is needed.
* An Encore server never offers the old "Get new music" request page, whatever the "Play songs your server does not have" switch says.
* The music server's door on the internet now slows wrong passwords like every other door (a stranger could try 65 a second); an app that sends only a plain password cannot sign in through the public address.
* A server that does not answer no longer makes the player skip songs: it waits and plays on when the server is back.
* One calm line, once, when the phone is offline or the server is away; "another server answers" gets its own line.
* A tap plays the full song, never a silent 30-second preview; the build says which build it is (About and Diagnostics).
* Downloads: a downloaded album or playlist is one entry that survives a restart; removing a playlist takes its songs off the phone at once; a song two downloaded releases share stays until both are gone; a per-song Download and Remove download in the song menu.
* Add to library and Download on a release are done by the server and show only what it confirmed.
* Long-press and a three-dots menu on songs and Library rows; one "my music / whole server" switch.
* Home offers Jam only where the server has Jam; the avatar opens a drawer; "Share what I'm listening to" only where it works; no speed control or Private listening in the player.
* Invitations: the link opens a page in the browser, a join whose answer was lost can be repeated, and a removed or locked person's phones, songs and pictures stop working.
* Wrong passwords are slowed and never lock an account; request sizes are limited; the server no longer publishes its route list.
* The first sign-in finds out within seconds that it is an Encore server; "Invite someone" and People appear once the server is known.
* The server installs in one command, backs itself up every night, and updates its own yt-dlp safely.
* The repository was reset: a clean history, GPL-3.0 licence.
