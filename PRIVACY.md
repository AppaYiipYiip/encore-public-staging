# Privacy

Encore has no central service. Your music, playlists, likes and listening history stay on **your own server**; the app talks only to
your own server.

What leaves your server, and only when you use the matching feature:

* Looking up songs that are not on your server: song titles and artist names are sent to YouTube / YouTube Music, Deezer, MusicBrainz and
  LRCLIB (public catalogues). No account, no history, no names of people.
* Recommendations: artist names are looked up in public catalogues (Deezer, MusicBrainz, ListenBrainz data). Your listening history is not sent.
* If you turn on "Friends on other Encore servers": only what each feature needs, and only after both people agree (see docs/13).

The Host of a server cannot see what members listen to or what is in their libraries. Details: [docs/21-security-privacy-and-roles.md](docs/21-security-privacy-and-roles.md).
