# Proposed additions (not scope): the owner accepts or rejects each

`docs/30` §0 question 1: a feature with no story is not built, it is filed. These come from the gap sweep of 3 Nov 2026 and from looking at what the app already has (checked in `app/lib`: a sleep timer, EQ presets, gapless playback, lyrics and a Year in Music card exist, and crossfade does not (only the sleep timer fades the volume; audit B-8); sharing a song as a link, playlist export, scrobbling, Android Auto and a home-screen widget do not). **None is scheduled until the owner says yes**; on the local roadmap board (`tools/roadmap_board.py`) they sit in the Inbox lane, and dragging one into the plan is saying yes: it becomes a story in `03a` plus a task with an Order line in the next session (until then it shows as "accepted, write-up pending").

| N-01 | to share a song, album or playlist as a link that opens in Encore (and a page for people without the app, like the invitation page) | The invitation page already shows how; sharing is how a music app spreads; no outside service is needed | M | quality and extras |
| N-02 | to export a playlist (M3U, CSV, JSON) and to leave with my data | "Your data" (P3-09) covers the account; playlist-level export is the portability a stranger expects before trusting a new app | S | with the host pages |
