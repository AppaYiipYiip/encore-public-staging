# History: dated records of what happened (NOT instructions)

Everything in this folder was true on the day it was written and is kept so that nobody has to guess how a decision came about. **Nothing here tells you what to do.** Where a record disagrees with a truth document (`docs/README.md` says which those are), the truth document wins. Do not edit a record to bring it up to date: add a new section, or put the new fact where it belongs (the decision log, the board's notes, the truth document).

| File | What it holds |
|------|---------------|
| `reviews-2026-10-31.md` | Five records: how a cloud session resumed the work (1 Nov 2026), the coherence audit of the documents, and the three QA reviews of 30 to 31 Oct 2026 (backend, round 1 with the owner's field test, the phone-app walk). Each old file name starts a section |
| `design-reviews.md` | The brief for the three directions, the choice of direction, the owner's review of the drawings (24 Oct 2026) and the review of every drawn screen against what was built (30 Oct 2026; the baseline of the app pass) |

Also history, kept where they are because other documents cite them by number: `docs/01-what-is-wrong.md` and `docs/02-questioning-the-premises.md` (the diagnosis of the old project), and the proofs in `docs/evidence/README.md`.

**A new history record is a new section at the end of one of these files, never a new markdown file.** Agents talk to each other through board notes (`roadmap.py note`), not through files.
