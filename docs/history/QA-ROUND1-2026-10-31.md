# QA round 1 and the owner's first field test (2026-10-31): findings and their triage

Round 1 was a testing agent on two emulators (build 123, the phone batch, against the integration build of every server branch). It found 26 things (7 defects, 11 confusing, 8 wishes) and could not run the friend and people walks because of R1-1. The same evening the owner used a phone outside for the first time and reported eight more. Triage follows D-29 (what blocks a real person on the main path is RC1; the rest is RC2 or the owner's own list). Everything marked "fixed" is **built and tested, not walked**: round 2 repeats it on a fresh install.

## What round 1 passed

Q1, Q2, Q30, F40 (online), D-26, D-32 passed as written. Q12 is "N songs in your library" on Your server, and it is there ("41 songs in your library"); R1-14 asked for a different thing (a label on kept songs in search) and is closed by design (no owned/catalogue labels). Q31 passed with the wording issues R1-9 and R1-10. Sign-in, sign-out, playlists (create, rename, add from an album and from search, delete), downloads, offline playing of a downloaded song, Now playing controls, the notification: no crash, no stuck state.

## Decisions (advisor, 2026-10-31, reversible)

* **D-45 The calm strip.** One outage, one sentence, shown once for a few seconds or until the cross, again only after a recovery; details live on Your server.
* **D-46 What "can reach the server" means for the player.** A network and not known to be unreachable; one look at the server before a refusal; a pin that is removed takes its songs off at once.
* **D-47 Phones stay upright** (shorter side under 600 dp); tablets and the PC turn freely; layouts for a phone on its side are RC2.
* The one-line testing rule that follows from the field test: **offline is not airplane mode.** Round 2 adds the states a person actually lives in: radio on with no data, the server stopped while Wi-Fi is on, a song ending into one that is not downloaded, a partly downloaded playlist, a deleted downloaded playlist (including an imported one), a ten-minute outage, and the owner's tiles on a cold start and after a fresh sign-in.
