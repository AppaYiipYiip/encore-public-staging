Why: Cover-led

Direction A, in seven lines.

The ideaThe music is the interface: each page takes its colour from the cover that plays, and the cover is the biggest thing on it.‎
Good atFeeling personal at a glance, familiar at once, and honest states told apart by words, icon and structure, not by hue.‎
Bad at200 % text, sunlight and text-heavy pages (the cover shrinks first); a page with no cover yet falls back to abstract art.‎
Flutter costPaletteGenerator in an isolate, ArtTint (InheritedWidget), TweenAnimationBuilder for the wash, Hero, SliverAppBar, ListView.builder with ResizeImage, FocusTraversalGroup with Shortcuts.‎
FramesNo blur and no shader animation: the cover wall and the bars are the only loops, transform only; to measure on a mid phone (docs/16 §4).‎
Others do betterB: long lists, 200 % text and sunlight. C: the first run of a person who has never heard of a server.‎
Borrow if A losesThe three state treatments, the focus drawing on T, and the colour wash on Now playing only.‎
