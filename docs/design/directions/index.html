Encore, updated 1 November 2026

How Encore will look

One direction, Spotify-like in colours and layout: the cover is the biggest thing on every page, and the screens people know (Now playing, Search, Library) are arranged as in the app they already use. Only the first-run screens, where Encore finds your server, have a personality of their own.

How to read these drawings

The drawings below were made on 24 and 25 October. The app has changed since, and the app as built is the reference for every screen that exists: it is arranged like Spotify's, which is the aim. A redraw of Now playing, the sleep timer, Your server and the first-run screens was tried on 31 October and not taken, so these files have not been redrawn.

Use them for pages that are not built yet (the installer wizard, the admin pages, the server's own front page) and as history. Where a screen exists, these are the known differences from the app:

  • Now playing draws a device icon and a share icon: the app has neither, because nothing is behind them yet.
  • The sleep timer and equalizer frames draw a playback speed control: the app has none.
  • Your server draws a "Testing…" button: the app has no such button. It checks each address by itself and shows its answer next to it.
  • Home draws your profile picture at the top right: in the app it is at the top left, as in Spotify, and opens the profile menu.
  • Settings, Notifications draws two phone categories, both off: the app still has three switches. Trimming them to two is planned for the second release candidate.
  • Devices, Share and Jam are not in this release: the app hides every way into them.
  • Home, "server not answering" draws an amber strip that stays, with a Try again button: the app says it once, for five seconds, with no button (D-45), and in the words of its cause ("You're offline" when Android found no internet, "Can't reach your server" when it found internet and the server is silent). The grey "Not downloaded" look of the offline frames is built (P1-15).
  • TV, tablet and car frames are parked: Encore is for phone and PC for now. The files keep those frames as history.

New pages are drawn only when they are about to be built, and only for phone and PC.

What you said, and what changed

  • Three buttons at the bottom (Home, Search, Your Library): kept.
  • Now playing is Spotify's: chevron, "Playing from", big cover, title and heart, scrubber, shuffle, previous, play, next, repeat, then the queue icon at the bottom. Lyrics appear as a card. The "Next:" card is gone. The drawing also has a device icon and a share icon. The app has neither, because nothing is behind them yet (decided on 30 October).
  • No "owned" or "catalogue" labels. Songs show only Spotify's icons: downloaded, liked, explicit. You do not need to know what is on the server.
  • The server is named for the household ("Our music"), never one person.
  • Welcome and "Your server" keep the friendly personality. "Your server" has "At home" and "Away from home", and the app has no test button (decided on 30 October): each address is checked by itself when the page opens and shows its own answer. The drawing still has the button.
  • Servers that do not talk to other servers simply do not show anything about friends elsewhere: no empty boxes, no greyed-out menus.
  • Comparing listening habits (you both like this artist, your favourite genre is that) is written down as a later feature, off by default, and only when both people say yes.
  • Adding a friend in Spotify means following them (Search, then Profiles, or their profile link or code). In Encore, people on your server are already there; others join with an invitation link.

The two other directions that were drawn first are dropped.

Decided for you while you were away

Everything the drawings left open was decided and written down (in docs/design/03). Tell me which ones to undo.

  • The admin has eight pages: Home, People, Library, Away from home, Backups, Updates, Settings, Logs. Backups and Updates stay two pages.
  • Recommendations shows in the admin only as a grey "later" line, not a switch, until they exist. The same goes for YouTube Music's data file when you bring your music.
  • The six-digit code is what you would type on a TV (parked); the invitation link carries a longer code you never type.
  • The phone shows no notifications unless you switch them on. News goes to the bell inside the app. The app still has three switches in Settings, Notifications where this decision allows two; they are trimmed after the first test rounds.
  • Tablet layouts are parked, like the TV, as your phone-and-PC decision says. The phone layout scales up.
  • The person chooses the streaming quality away from home, as in Spotify; the server's owner may cap it, and the app mentions a cap only when there is one.
  • No person's name in a server's pages ("Our music", a folder called Host).

What happens next

  1. The first release candidate is tested as the app is built now. Restyling every screen first would make the test meaningless. Afterwards a screen changes only when there is a reason, and it stays arranged like Spotify's.
  2. New pages, such as the installer wizard and the server's front page, are drawn only when they are about to be built, and only for phone and PC.
  3. The second release candidate builds the pages that do not exist yet. In this order: the new person's Home and the album wall on the Welcome pages, the installer wizard, the server's front page, the admin pages, a person's profile and account, and Recently played.

If a screen feels wrong, tell me which one.
